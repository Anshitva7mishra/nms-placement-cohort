# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

NMS exists to reduce struggle for students. The team works in the same spirit: respectful, honest and supportive.

## Expected Behavior

1. Be respectful in reviews and discussions. Critique the work, not the person.
2. Be honest. Do not exaggerate results, testimonials or company associations.
3. Protect privacy. Student and mentor data stays confidential.
4. Give credit. Mentor experiences and shared material are attributed to their authors.
5. Keep discussions inclusive of all branches, colleges and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable Behavior

1. Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
2. Sharing private student or mentor information.
3. Publishing misleading claims about placements, packages or partnerships.
4. Copying paid course material or third-party content without permission.

## Enforcement

Concerns can be raised with the Program Lead. The team will review the matter privately and decide on an appropriate action, which may include removal of repository access.
