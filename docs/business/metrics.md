# Success Metrics

| Metric | Why it matters | Target / Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Registrations per stream | Demand across Combined Tech (170–200) and Mechanical (80–100) | 250 to 300 total students (Cohort 1) |
| Attendance and assignment completion | Measure engagement with live masterclasses and practice sets | >85% weekly completion |
| AI Assistant resolution efficiency | 24/7 instant doubt clearing without bottlenecking instructors | <5 min resolution for code doubts, >90% helpfulness |
| Baseline vs final assessment score | Quantify real skill delta across DSA, core CS, and Mechanical subjects | >40% average score improvement |
| Mock interview rating growth | Measure interview readiness across standard rubric | ≥3.5 / 5 average rating by Week 10 |
| Certificate qualification rate | Percentage of students satisfying verifiable certificate exit criteria | >75% of enrolled cohort |
| Interviews attended and verified offers | Track employment and internship conversion without false promises | Documented in verified outcome reports |
| Satisfaction (NPS) and referrals | Organic word-of-mouth strength | NPS > 60; >20% referral contribution |

## Funnel Metrics

| Stage | Metric |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Content reach across LinkedIn, Instagram, and college communities (2nd & 3rd years) |
| Interest | Free resume review and masterclass webinar sign-ups |
| Trial | Demo mock interview and AI assistant sample interaction |
| Conversion | Enrollment into Cohort 1 (targeting 250–300 seats) |
| Retention | Weekly mini-test submission and mock interview attendance |
| Certification | Award of NMS Certificate of Completion with unique Verification ID |
| Institutional Readiness | Outcome dataset compiled for college MoUs and AICTE/NSDC pathway (ADR 0003) |

## Measurement Rules

1. Use standard diagnostic baseline and final assessments for all students in a stream
2. Use the unified mock interview rubric across all evaluators
3. Report outcomes strictly as audited, tracked data—never as a marketing guarantee
4. Collect written consent before publishing any testimonial, score, or placement story
5. Review metrics weekly in the Sunday team sync

## Review Cadence

| Frequency | Review |
|---|---|
| Weekly | Attendance, assignments, mini test scores |
| Mid-cohort | Feedback survey and mentor review |
| End of cohort | Baseline vs final, outcomes, satisfaction |
