# ADR 0001: No Placement Guarantee

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-05
- Deciders: NMS core team

## Context

Many placement programs advertise guaranteed placement. This is legally and ethically risky, and it conflicts with NMS's positioning as an honest, mentor-led cohort.

## Options Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Offer a placement guarantee | Strong marketing hook | Legal risk, trust risk, cannot control outcomes |
| Income-share or placement-linked fees | Aligns incentives | Complex, risky, implies a guarantee |
| Honest roadmap with outcome tracking | Builds long-term trust, low legal risk | Less aggressive marketing |

## Decision

NMS will not offer or imply any placement guarantee. Outcomes are shown as tracked data (interviews attended, offers received, score improvement), not as promises.

## Consequences

1. All marketing, ads and mentor communication avoid guarantee language
2. Pricing is not linked to placement outcomes
3. Trust is built through free sessions, transparency and testimonials with consent
4. Pull requests that add guarantee language should be rejected
