# ADR 0002: Cohort 1 Scale, Combined Tech Track, AI Assistant, and Target Audience

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-05
- Deciders: NMS core team

## Context

As NMS prepares for the launch of Cohort 1, key architectural, curriculum, and operational questions required definition:
1. Target intake size for the founding cohort.
2. Track organization across engineering and computer applications branches.
3. Scalable delivery and mentorship model capable of maintaining personal attention without prohibitive mentor overhead.
4. Target student year priority (1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year).

## Decisions

### 1. Cohort 1 Target Size: 250 to 300 Students
The target enrollment for Cohort 1 is set to **250 to 300 students**. This gives NMS critical mass, robust community interaction, and meaningful statistical validation of the curriculum.

### 2. Track Consolidation: Combined Tech Track and Separate Mechanical Track
- **Combined Tech Track (CS/IT + BCA/MCA):** Computer Science, Information Technology, BCA, and MCA students will run **together** in a unified tech track. Foundational leveling will bridge beginners and BCA/MCA students to intermediate problem-solving before advancing to advanced DSA and core CS.
- **Separate Mechanical Track:** Mechanical engineering students will run as a **dedicated, independent track** focusing on core mechanical subjects, design/CAD tools, aptitude, and company-specific tests.

### 3. Delivery Model: Teacher + AI Assistant
To scale 250 to 300 students while maintaining personal attention and rapid feedback:
- **Lead Teacher(s):** Conducts live core concept sessions, system design masterclasses, complex problem-solving walk-throughs, and strategic direction.
- **AI Assistant:** Provides 24/7 on-demand support including:
  - Instant code debugging and conceptual doubt resolution.
  - Automated diagnostic assessment grading and error analysis.
  - ATS resume scanning and initial feedback.
  - Practice mock interview prompts, technical drills, and response rubrics.
- **Human Evaluators / TAs:** Support weekend mock interviews and spot-check written reviews.

### 4. Target Audience: Majorly 2nd and 3rd Year Students
- **Primary Audience (70–80%):** **2nd and 3rd year students**. These students have adequate runway to build strong foundations, target summer internships, and prepare for on-campus/off-campus placements without eleventh-hour panic.
- **Secondary Audience (20–30%):** 
  - **4th year students:** Fast-track preparation for immediate off-campus and service-to-product transitions.
  - **1st year students:** Early foundation builders seeking programming clarity.

## Consequences

1. Curriculum documentation for CS/IT and BCA/MCA is unified under a tiered Tech curriculum.
2. The mentorship and operations model is updated from a purely manual 1:4 mentor structure to a scalable **Teacher + AI Assistant** framework.
3. Marketing, college outreach, and GTM channels will prioritize 2nd and 3rd year college students.
4. Tooling evaluation must include AI assistant integration (e.g. custom bot, Discord/Slack integration, or API-backed platform).
