# Common Modules (All Streams)

These core modules run across both the **Combined Tech Track** and the **Independent Mechanical Track**.

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| Resume and LinkedIn makeover | ATS-friendly resume formatting with AI-assisted review and mentor feedback |
| Mock interviews | Recorded 1-on-1 sessions on weekends with standardized rubric scoring and written feedback |
| Placement Playbook | Verified, real-world interview experiences from recently placed graduates |
| Communication workshops | Self-introduction, HR behavioural questions, and project presentation masterclasses |
| Peer Accountability Circles | Groups of 4 to 5 students supported by the AI Assistant and student TAs for weekly target tracking |
| Verified Certification | NMS Certificate of Completion with unique verification ID under Udyam MSME registration (ADR 0003) |

## Mock Interview Rubric

| Criterion | What is assessed | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Technical depth | Correctness, core subject understanding, and algorithmic clarity | 1 to 5 |
| Problem solving | Structured thinking, edge-case analysis, and reasoning | 1 to 5 |
| Communication | Clarity, articulation, structured responses, and confidence | 1 to 5 |
| Project / Subject ownership | Genuine depth of project code, design choices, and trade-offs | 1 to 5 |
| Professionalism | Preparation, body language, and interview etiquette | 1 to 5 |

## Feedback Format

1. Identified Strengths
2. Areas Requiring Immediate Improvement
3. Actionable Targets for the Upcoming Week
4. Score ratings per rubric criterion (logged to student verification profile)

## Placement Playbook Entry Template

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Company & Role | e.g. SDE-1, Graduate Engineer Trainee |
| Stream | Combined Tech or Mechanical |
| Hiring Rounds | Online assessment (OA), technical rounds, HR round |
| Questions & Topics Asked | Exact technical questions, problem variations, system/design questions |
| Common Traps & Mistakes | Where candidates usually stumble |
| Preparation Strategy | Recommended resources and approach |
| Verified By | Evaluator/Mentor name and verification date |

## Peer Accountability Circles

Small groups of 4 to 5 students check in weekly to review assignment completion, troubleshoot common blockers, and hold each other accountable, with 24/7 support from the AI Assistant and escalation to the Lead Teacher.

## Consent

Recordings and testimonials are used only with explicit written consent.
